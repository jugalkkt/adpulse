## Detection
- Expected alert: `AdPulseDatabaseDown` → **fired**, MTTD **22.1 s**.
- Other alerts seen: none
- Was it the right alert? Yes. It stayed firing (fixes from the first staging db-down RCA), the inhibition kept ServingFallbackAds quiet, and the healer acted within one `group_wait` of firing.

## Root cause
Injected fault: the database container was stopped. Prod has more traffic (25 rps) over more category/segment keys than the probe exercises, so many requests missed the 60 s cache and needed the DB, which was down: house ads after the 0.5 s DB timeout (p95 0.861 s).

## 5 Whys
1. Why 24.5% house ads? Requests for keys not in the cache needed the DB.
2. Why so many misses? 25 rps spread over 30 keys, each cached for only 60 s after its last DB read.
3. Why did the probe show much less? It always asks for the same key, which stayed cached.
4. Why did healing work in prod? keep_firing_for and fast-fail DNS (from the staging RCA) applied to every env via the same Terraform module.
5. Why 40 s? 22 s detection + 10 s group_wait + ~14 s restart and checks (overlapping).

## Resolution
- Healer: `restart_db` → **success** in 13.79 s (attempt 1, vars `{"env": "prod", "alertname": "AdPulseDatabaseDown"}`).
- Expected heal: restart_db.
- **MTTR: 40.4 s** (injection → system healthy and 3 consecutive good probes).

## What went well / What went badly / Where we got lucky
**Went well:** the staging lessons transferred to prod unchanged (same module, same rules); a fast, automatic, verified recovery.
**Went badly:** a quarter of prod impressions were house ads for 40 s, which is real money in an ad business.
**Lucky:** the outage was shorter than the cache TTL for the hottest keys.
